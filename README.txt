DARK FORTRESS v2 - Fabric mod for Minecraft Java 1.21.1

USE A BRAND-NEW WORLD. On first load the mod builds a 97x97 fortress at world spawn
(can take 30-90 seconds; on a dedicated server set max-tick-time=-1 in server.properties).
Gamerules changed: spawnRadius 0, keepInventory on, natural mob spawning off
(edit constants at top of DarkFortressMod.java).

BUILD: push to GitHub; the Actions workflow builds the jar (artifact "darkfortress-jar").

WHAT'S NEW IN v2
 - Resting at a bonfire OR respawning repairs all your gear and refills Estus.
 - No enemies spawn within 10 blocks of any bonfire. 6 bonfires in the fortress.
 - Loot chests everywhere (3 loot tiers).
 - Weapon classes: swords (Longsword, Claymore), dagger (backstab), hatchet (breaks shields),
   spear (reach), katana (bleed), halberd (reach + cleave), warhammer (stun), Soul Staff,
   Firebomb, Pyromancy Flame, Lightning Spear, plus Green Blossom.
 - New 3D worn armor: Knight set and Black Knight set.
 - New enemies: Skeleton Warrior, Hollow Knight, Ash Ghoul, Hollow Mage.
 - New rooms: Barracks, Chapel, Belfry, Graveyard + mausoleum, Market, Ossuary, Prison, Armory.
 - New bosses: Stone Gargoyle (Belfry), Witch of Ash (Chapel), Labyrinth Warden (Ashen Realm).
 - Beat the Demon of the Keep and a portal opens to the ASHEN REALM: an endless twilit
   dimension with labyrinths, ruined chapels, watchtowers, graveyards and bonfire obelisks.
   A return pad is at the realm's hub.
